## Behavior and tuning

- `work-order.js`: three-height sections, at most128 placements in a supply preview;
  greedy nearest placement selection within layers up to512 blocks. Larger layers
  retain their ordering. Placement support and door access still take priority.
- `supplies.js`: up to four material types per phase, two free slots retained,
  targets of64 raw/32 crafted items, at most16 recipe repetitions. Recipes retain
  server-acknowledged inventory transactions; these are not artificial tick boosts.
- `raw-materials.js`: use the existing64-block worksite radius and16-block mining
  batches. Ask permitted chests for plain iron tools, then select the fastest usable
  owned tool with at least16 durability remaining. No replacement-tool crafting or
  spending rare shared materials. Unavailable gear can still block construction.
- `resource-search.js`: explicit searches default to256 blocks and48 safe surface
  waypoints, expanding from nearby terrain in stages. Require daylight, health, food, no nearby hostiles,
  and approved storage for valuable cargo. Save visible source coordinates, return,
  and report observation limits. A distant jungle, portal travel or mob-drop farming
  remains outside this skill. Search does not itself authorize harvesting.
- `material-requests.js`: missing materials are saved per project revision, explained
  in Shared tasks and announced once. Repeated checks do not spam the same request.
  Resuming supply checks reconciles sufficient observed inventory. User-assigned
  shares remain protected unless the construction plan permits taking them over.
- `streaming.js`: sentence chunks target180 characters. Local speech weights remain
  resident during an active session; pause/stop releases them. The first activation
  still loads the installed models. Browser and desktop clients preserve output
  selection, volume and interruption. No private audio is stored.
- `performance.js`: last100 samples per fixed stage, counts/failures, median/P95.
  Session-local timings can overlap and reset on backend restart. No model calls,
  private content or billing estimates are generated by diagnostics.

## Extended searches — 2026-10-30

`SEARCH_LIMITS` in `services/skill-executor/search-route.js` centralizes expedition limits:
256-block maximum radius,48 waypoints, four minutes outward/ten minutes total,15 seconds
per navigation segment, eight food items for longer trips and a1200-tick daylight margin
plus a conservative return-time estimate. Resource searches have a dedicated eleven-minute
action timeout; other gameplay retains its existing45-second cap.

Before departure, store observed valuable cargo in an approved deposit chest. Protected,
reserved or rejected valuables still in inventory prevent departure. Useful equipment is
retained. Longer searches start from saved Home/Base, or confirmed server spawn when Home is unset, in the same Realm/dimension, nearby
with an observed clear route. Without that preparation, remain within64 blocks and explain
what is missing. Search scans from the bot as new chunks load, without invoking the model.

Only observed flat surface corridors with solid footing and clear headroom are accepted.
No digging, portals, swimming or cliff traversal. Save actual grounded movement breadcrumbs
outside Git, retrace them in reverse and recheck each segment. If the route changes, becomes
unloaded or unsafe, save the remaining route and ask for help. Pause/disconnect stops movement;
the next search request recovers the saved route before scouting anew. Return is confirmed
before claiming arrival. Dynamic mobs/terrain still make an absolute survival guarantee
impossible. Source discovery remains separate from harvesting permission.


Validation for the extended search: eight dedicated expedition cases cover distance,
food/home requirements, valuables without storage, early return, pause/recovery,
blocked/unloaded routes, holes, daylight and Realm scope. The official localhost
survival fixture confirmed three diamonds deposited in an approved chest, discovered
bamboo84.85 blocks from Home and verified return with the checkpoint route cleared.
A source off the selected route may be missed: this is bounded scouting, not exhaustive
coverage of every block within the radius. Typecheck, production build and the full
suite passed (260 cases:257 passed, three optional live/provider/speech cases skipped).
The existing headless dashboard integration also passed. No personal Realm was used.


## Home/spawn deposit policy — 2026-10-30

Loot deposits require approved deposit-enabled chests within `HOME_STORAGE_RADIUS`
(32 blocks) of saved Home/Base. Only when Home/Base is absent does server-reported
spawn become the fallback. Spawn positions are observed from `spawn_position` protocol
packets and persisted by Realm/dimension; default library coordinates and login
positions are never treated as confirmed spawn. A Home in another dimension requires
returning there. Deposits remain locally bounded (64 blocks from the bot), with the
policy rechecked after navigation and before transfers. Unavailable storage leaves
items in inventory. Saved fractional Home positions normalize to their actual block.

Validation: six policy cases, existing survival/search checks, full typecheck/build/tests,
headless teamwork UI, and actual localhost survival deposits of three diamonds at
confirmed spawn with no Home saved, followed by distant bamboo scouting and return.
Personal Realm gameplay remains untested for this policy.

## Self-sufficient cooking — 2026-10-30

Food searches cook their raw catch automatically. Productive mode prioritizes raw food
and recoverable owned furnace batches even when the food target is already met. Cooking
can prepare a wooden pickaxe, gather eight cobblestone from safe exposed Overworld rock,
craft/place a furnace, and obtain common wood fuel. Coal/charcoal already owned is used
when sufficient; coal mining is unnecessary. Saved stone observations are retained while
moving to prepare tools. Fuel yields and stock reservations are checked before loading.

`COOKING_LIMITS`:32-block source observation radius, four scouting steps within48 blocks,
12 maximum stone removals, eight items per batch. No below-foot excavation; immediate
Home/spawn, known chests and saved build footprints are protected. Cooking has the same
four-minute bounded action allowance as independent supply jobs. Inputs and output are
server-confirmed. Occupied foreign furnaces are skipped; own interrupted jobs resume only
when phase, Realm/dimension, input/output quantities and fuel match the checkpoint.

Verification:276 automated cases (273 passed,3 optional provider/speech cases skipped),
typecheck/build and headless teammate UI passed. Isolated official26.3 survival tests
passed three cooking cases (full preparation from raw meat/wood, pause/resume, busy-foreign
furnace isolation), one complete hunting-to-cooked-food case, and seven existing survival
regressions. An initial crop interruption fixture was timing-sensitive under concurrent
local servers; its sequential rerun passed without farming changes. No destructive
personal Realm experiment was performed.


## Automatic equipment upkeep (2026-10-30)

`gear-upkeep.js` plans personal upgrades without model requests. Available material
budgets respect reservations and recently observed approved supplies. Carried plain
diamonds may be used for personal equipment; shared diamonds are never withdrawn.
Crafting uses the connected version's recipes and confirmed server inventories.
Equipped armor participates in ownership checks, preventing duplicate armor loops.

The default-on `autoGear` preference is independent of loot storage. Idle upkeep
requires productive mode, safe surroundings and a food reserve. It smelts owned raw
iron, makes wooden/stone/iron/diamond tools and leather/iron/diamond armor, and equips
upgrades while preserving usable named/enchanted gear. Netherite smithing, copper
gear crafting, enchanting and anvil repair remain outside this controller.

Collecting checks worn tools before each target. Near the 8%/16-use threshold it
confirms durability, since dig completion can precede server wear packets. A focused
survival regression reproduces threshold crossing: the original iron axe stops at
20 uses remaining and a crafted replacement finishes the tree. Tool preparation is
scoped to the collector; generic tool selection cannot unexpectedly leave a custom
construction route. Harvest counts exclude crafting outputs and ingredient spending.
Storage retains a usable primary plus a spare; worn expendable gear can be deposited.
All work remains inside the existing cancellation/controller boundary.

Validation: automated policy/reservation/cancellation checks; a headless dashboard
preference test with reload persistence and desktop/mobile overflow checks; three
local survival gear scenarios (iron kit, mid-job replacement, raw-iron smelting),
and the three cooking regression scenarios. No personal Realm gameplay is claimed.
Evidence is kept under `.git/gear-game-results.json`, `.git/cooking-game-results.json`,
`.git/verification/teammate-ui/` and `.git/hackers-lair/verification.json`.

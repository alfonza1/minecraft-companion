No movement cheats, invented inventory, public service exposure or paid providers.
Speech can start sentence playback while later audio is generated; model-response
streaming depends on the configured provider interface and must never execute
partially parsed actions. Hard-material searches do not promise distant biomes,
unsafe loot farming or harvesting someone else's crops.

## Behavior and tuning

- `work-order.js`: three-height sections, at most128 placements in a supply preview;
  greedy nearest placement selection within layers up to512 blocks. Larger layers
  retain their ordering. Placement support and door access still take priority.
- `supplies.js`: up to four material types per phase, two free slots retained,
  targets of64 raw/32 crafted items, at most16 recipe repetitions. Recipes retain
  server-acknowledged inventory transactions; these are not artificial tick boosts.
- `raw-materials.js`: use the existing64-block worksite radius and16-block mining
  batches. Ask permitted chests for plain iron tools, then select the fastest usable
  owned tool with at least16 durability remaining. No replacement-tool crafting or
  spending rare shared materials. Unavailable gear can still block construction.
- `resource-search.js`: explicit searches default to48 blocks and six safe surface
  waypoints, capped at64 blocks. Require daylight, health, food, no nearby hostiles,
  and approved storage for valuable cargo. Save visible source coordinates, return,
  and report observation limits. A distant jungle, portal travel or mob-drop farming
  remains outside this skill. Search does not itself authorize harvesting.
- `material-requests.js`: missing materials are saved per project revision, explained
  in Shared tasks and announced once. Repeated checks do not spam the same request.
  Resuming supply checks reconciles sufficient observed inventory. User-assigned
  shares remain protected unless the construction plan permits taking them over.
- `streaming.js`: sentence chunks target180 characters. Local speech weights remain
  resident during an active session; pause/stop releases them. The first activation
  still loads the installed models. Browser and desktop clients preserve output
  selection, volume and interruption. No private audio is stored.
- `performance.js`: last100 samples per fixed stage, counts/failures, median/P95.
  Session-local timings can overlap and reset on backend restart. No model calls,
  private content or billing estimates are generated by diagnostics.
